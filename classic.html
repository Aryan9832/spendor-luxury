Heritage

The BBC Legacy

The Classic series draws directly from our founder Spencer Hughes' work at the BBC. These speakers embody the principles established with the legendary BC1 studio monitor – natural tonal balance, accurate midrange, and long-term listening comfort.

Each Classic loudspeaker is designed to deliver music the way artists and engineers intended it to be heard. The rich, warm tonality combined with precise detail retrieval creates an immersive listening experience that reveals new layers in familiar recordings.

Hand-built in our UK facility using traditional cabinet-making techniques and modern acoustic engineering, Classic speakers represent the perfect marriage of heritage and innovation.
